Engineering Pillars

Built for efficiency, acoustic precision, and tactile control

ZERO-SHOT [CH-01]

In-Context Voice Cloning

Drop any 5–30 second audio clip or record live via your browser microphone. The model extracts speaker timbre, formant contours, and vocal traits on-the-fly without fine-tuning weights.

1.6-BIT TERNARY [CH-02]

1.7B Speech Language Model

Extreme ternary quantization packing down to ~1.03 GB disk footprint. Executes comfortably within 1.2 GB – 1.5 GB of CPU RAM, completely avoiding GPU memory exhaustion.

EMOTION SYNTAX [CH-03]

Expression & Emotion Tags

Infuse personality with inline speech tags: [happy], [whisper], [excited], [serious], non-speech vocal triggers like <laugh>, and (((emphasis))).
