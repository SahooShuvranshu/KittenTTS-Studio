Engineering Pillars

Built for efficiency, privacy, and acoustic precision

In-Context Voice Cloning

Upload any 5–30 second audio clip or record live via your browser microphone. The model extracts speaker timbre and pitch contours on-the-fly with zero fine-tuning.

1.6-bit Ternary Weights

The 1.7B parameter speech language model uses extreme ternary quantization, shrinking the core weights to ~1.03 GB and running smoothly within ~1.2 GB of CPU RAM.

Expression & Emotion Syntax

Control delivery with inline tags: [happy], [whisper], [serious], non-speech events like <laugh>, and (((emphasis))) spans.
