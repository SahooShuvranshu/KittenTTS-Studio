Expression & Emotion Prosody Playbook

Kitten-TTS 2 parses speech prompt conditioning tags, paralinguistic vocal events, and emphasis punctuation natively inside prompt text without requiring external phoneme dictionaries or SSML tags.

1. How In-Context Prosody Conditioning Works

Unlike legacy speech engines that require cumbersome W3C SSML (Speech Synthesis Markup Language) tags like <prosody pitch="+5st">, Kitten-TTS 2 treats emotion and paralinguistic tags as specialized semantic tokens directly embedded in the autoregressive language modeling sequence.

When the neural speech decoder encounters a tag such as [whisper] or <laugh>, it modulates the acoustic latent representation, shifting glottal aperture, vocal fold tension, and harmonic formant trajectories in real time.

2. Emotion & Mood Prosody Tags

Prepend or embed emotion tags within your text to guide the tonal delivery:

Tag Acoustic Inflection Vocal Tract Dynamics Example Prompt Action
[happy] Warm, upbeat, energetic cadence Elevated F0 pitch contour with wider harmonic resonance [happy] Good morning! The system telemetry looks fantastic today.
[whisper] Quiet, breathy, intimate delivery Attenuated glottal closure, elevated aspiration noise [whisper] Keep your voice down, the servers are sleeping.
[serious] Authoritative, firm, steady cadence Compressed pitch variance, deliberate pause timing [serious] Critical threshold reached. Initiating protocol zero.
[excited] High tempo with rapid pitch peaks Elevated fundamental frequency, sharp energy bursts [excited] We just benchmarked over 1.2x real-time generation on CPU!

3. Paralinguistic Vocal Events

Paralinguistic events synthesize non-verbal human vocalizations directly within conversational speech:

Event Acoustic Gesture Generated Sound Example Prompt Action
<laugh> Rhythmic breath bursts & pitch chuckles Audible chuckling / laughter You really thought that would fail? <laugh> It compiled on the first try.
<sigh> Prolonged audible exhalation & cadence drop Tired or contemplative sigh Another 3:00 AM debugging session. <sigh> Back to the terminal.
💡 Placement Tip for <laugh> and <sigh>

Place <laugh> and <sigh> between punctuation marks (e.g. ? <laugh> It or . <sigh> Back) to allow natural pre-event inhalation and post-event pitch recovery.

4. Prosodic Emphasis & Punctuation Nuance

Fine-tune stress, rhythm, and pauses using simple punctuation rules:

Syntax Rule Acoustic Effect Example Prompt
(((word))) Heavy stress with elevated amplitude & pitch peak This parameter is (((crucial))) for memory stability.
... (Ellipsis) Cadence hesitation with gentle pitch drop (~350ms pause) The model is loading... Stand by for audio.
-- (Double Dash) Abrupt pause with immediate glottal stop (~150ms break) Wait-- did the benchmark really drop below 0.10x RTF?
! (Exclamation) Elevated final pitch contour and shorter phoneme duration Synthesis complete!

5. Multi-Tag Complex Dialogue Compositions

Combine emotional tags, paralinguistic events, and emphasis to craft nuanced, human-sounding speech flows:

PROMPT // TECHNICAL SUPPORT WORKSTATION
[happy] Welcome back to the audio lab! <laugh> I see you've already provisioned the new 24kHz pipeline. [serious] Just make sure you keep the working set (((under))) 1.4 gigabytes.
PROMPT // AUDIOBOOK SUSPENSE SCENE
[whisper] Don't move... I hear footsteps right outside the server door. <sigh> If they touch the power cable, (((everything))) goes dark.
