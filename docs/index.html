◈ Hardware & System Architecture

Kitten-TTS 2 Studio addresses the physical constraints of everyday consumer hardware. It bypasses memory-constrained laptop GPUs entirely and delivers real-time speech synthesis on the CPU using AVX2 vectorization.

[SYS_01] Model Compression
1.6-bit Ternary Weights
Quantized down to ~1.03 GB disk footprint, enabling real-time neural speech synthesis in ~1.2 GB of CPU RAM.
[SYS_02] Audio Ingestion
24 kHz Mono Pipeline
Automated FFmpeg transcoding converts incoming MP3, M4A, FLAC, and WebM into clean 16-bit PCM WAV.
[SYS_03] Acoustic Engine
Zero-Shot Formant Extraction
Captures speaker identity and vocal idiosyncrasies dynamically without any gradient fine-tuning.
